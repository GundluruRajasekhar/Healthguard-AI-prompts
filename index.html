              Fall detection

              Uses your phone's motion sensor. A short free-fall, a hard impact and then stillness starts a 15-second countdown. If you do not tap "I'm OK", an SOS goes out.

              Medical report analysis

              Paste lab results, one per line, like "LDL 165 mg/dL". The Medical Records agent compares each value with standard adult ranges.

                Reference ranges vary by lab, age and sex. This is an explanation aid, not a diagnosis. Review every result with your doctor.

                Encrypted vault

                Locks your records with AES-256-GCM. The key comes from your passphrase (PBKDF2, 210,000 rounds) and never leaves this browser. Only the ciphertext is saved on this device.
